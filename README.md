# Office Joke — Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open

Entry for make.fun competition
`u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232`.

`main.py` writes one single-line joke about office life to
`/output/entry.txt` (85 characters, under the 200-character cap):

> I hit reply-all to say 'please stop replying all' and 47 people replied all to agree.

The joke is deterministic: every run writes the exact same bytes.
